Tell us which iPhone or iPad you're using, your macOS version, your Quad Cortex firmware version, and what happened. If the Mac bridge shows an error, use **Export report…** in its window and attach the file. Reports leave out your preset contents.

## What you need

- An iPhone or iPad with Bluetooth.
- A Mac running the free Patchbridge bridge app.
- A Quad Cortex connected to that Mac over USB.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Why do I need a Mac?</summary>

The Quad Cortex connects over USB. The Mac bridge holds that connection and talks to your iPhone over Bluetooth.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My iPhone can't find my Mac</summary>

Make sure the bridge app is open on the Mac, Bluetooth is on for both devices, and they're within a few meters of each other. Then tap **Find my Mac** again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The Mac bridge doesn't see my Quad Cortex</summary>

Quit Cortex Control, since only one app can use the USB connection at a time. Then unplug the QC's USB cable and plug it back in.

</details>

<details>
<summary>An edit says "Not applied"</summary>

The QC didn't accept the change, usually because something changed on the unit, like the scene. The value shown is what the QC actually holds. Try the edit again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are my edits saved?</summary>

Not until you tap **Save**. Until then you'll see **Unsaved** next to the preset name, just like on the QC.

</details>
