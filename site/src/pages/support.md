If you're having an issue with Patchbridge, email patchbridge@arjunakankipati.com. Include information about which iPhone or iPad you're using, your macOS version, your Quad Cortex firmware version, and a general error description. If the Mac bridge reports an error, use **Export report…** and attach the file. Reports leave out your preset contents.

## What you need to run Patchbridge

- An iPhone or iPad with Bluetooth, running the free Patchbridge app.
- A Mac with Bluetooth, running the free Patchbridge bridge app.
- A Quad Cortex connected to that Mac over USB.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Why do I need a Mac?</summary>

The Quad Cortex connects over USB. The Mac bridge reads data from the QC and talks to the iOS App over Bluetooth, and also writes your changes back to the QC.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My iPhone can't find my Mac</summary>

Make sure the bridge app is open on the Mac, Bluetooth is enabled on both devices, and they're within a few meters of each other. Then tap **Find my Mac** again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The Mac bridge doesn't see my Quad Cortex</summary>

Check if Cortex Control is still open. If so, close it and unplug and replug your QC. Only one app can talk to the QC over USB at a time. 

</details>

<details>
<summary>An edit says "Not applied"</summary>

The QC rejected the change, often because something changed on the unit, like the scene. Try the edit again, and if it continues to fail, email us with the intended edit so we can investigate.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are my edits saved to a profile?</summary>

Not until you hit **Save**. Edits are live so you can hear the change until you are happy and choose to save the change(s) to the QC.

</details>
