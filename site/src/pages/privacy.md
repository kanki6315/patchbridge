This policy covers the Patchbridge app for iPhone and iPad and the Patchbridge bridge app for Mac (together, "Patchbridge").

## Information we collect

None. Patchbridge has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We don't run servers that receive data from the app.

## How the app uses your data

Patchbridge reads presets and settings from your Quad Cortex through the Mac bridge so you can view and edit them. This data moves only between your own devices, over USB and Bluetooth. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The app stores a few preferences, such as your theme and which Mac you approved, on your device.

## Diagnostic reports

The Mac bridge can export a diagnostic report when you choose **Export report…**. The report is saved on your Mac and leaves out your preset contents and identifiers. It is only shared if you choose to send it to us, for example by email. We use it only to fix the problem you reported.

## Email

If you email us for support, we use your address and message only to reply to you.

## Children

Patchbridge isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
